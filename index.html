How I Work

I work at the intersection of trust and usability, turning complex problems into experiences that feel clear, effortless, and genuinely usable for everyone.

With a large appetite for learning, I find the interplay of design, psychology and technology extremely intriguing.

As the sole designer on complex, regulated products, I lead through collaboration, working directly with PMs, engineers, security and compliance, the service desk, and QA to make the right trade-offs between hard constraints and the user experience.

I believe the best design comes from understanding constraints deeply enough to design within them gracefully, rather than fighting against them. It's grounded in research, testing, and iteration. I own the design system, ship across three platforms, and write copy that survives translation into eight languages.

  • Regulated digital identity depth: eIDAS, qualified electronic signatures, eHerkenning, and the EU Digital Identity Wallet. Fluent in the compliance logic, not just the screens.
